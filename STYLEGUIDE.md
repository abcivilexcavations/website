# ABCivil Website Style Guide

## Brand colours

- Primary green: `#114A4F`
- Dark green: `#0B383C`
- CTA orange: `#E67E22`
- Dark text: `#0E1111`
- Secondary text: `#6F7777`
- Light background: `#F3F5F4`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Orange is reserved for high-value actions, especially **Get a Free Quote**.

## Typography

- Headings: Poppins, weights 600 and 700
- Body: Inter, weights 400, 500 and 600

## Components

- Buttons use an 8px radius and subtle shadow.
- Cards use a 10px radius, light border and restrained lift-on-hover.
- Main content width is capped at 1200px.
- Sections use generous vertical spacing.
- Animations are subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Photography

Use genuine ABCivil photography wherever possible. Images should show machinery, operators and real sites. Optimise to WebP before publishing.
